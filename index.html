Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and billing

What services does Para Tube offer?

Para Tube provides high-speed internet, premium cable TV, home phone service, and business connectivity solutions. As a certified dealer, we partner with leading providers to give you quality options.

What companies are serviceable through Para Tube?

We partner with major providers including AT&T, Verizon, Spectrum, Comcast Xfinity, Frontier, HughesNet, CenturyLink, and many more. We ensure diverse, reliable options for everyone.

Is Para Tube a service provider?

We’re an authorized dealer and certified retailer. We work with leading service providers to connect you to the best offerings while providing expert support.

What are Para Tube’s execution charges?

Our execution services (setup, bill payments, plan upgrades, cancellations) range from $49.99 to $199.99, separate from your provider’s billing for the actual service.

Why are charges separate from my provider’s billing?

As an independent entity, Para Tube charges for execution services (setup, upgrades, account management) while your provider bills for the actual internet, TV, or phone service.

How do I contact Para Tube for support?

Reach us by phone at (888) 597-3167 or use the contact form on this page. Our support team is available 24/7 for any questions or service requests.
